Statement

I build each piece from a feeling first, merging figurative forms with organic elements to reveal the internal landscapes we usually hide. These paintings aren’t meant to explain anything; they’re meant to slow you down. About the artist

The newest body of work. Faces layered over faces, painted in flat, cut-paper color. What we carry underneath, and how it surfaces when no one is looking.

Figures dissolving into landscape and back again. Raw canvas, saturated color, and the feeling of a season passing faster than you can hold it.

A monochrome series on raw canvas. Bodies and faces pulled from the subconscious in black acrylic, the way a memory arrives before you can name it.

The earliest series. Dense, jewel-toned compositions where several people share one head. Obsession, attention, and the crowd inside a single mind.
